## [1.1.0](https://github.com/corentin-gautier/restyle-for-twitch/compare/v1.0.1...v1.1.0) (2026-10-10)

### Features

* restyle the offline channel page, the video page and its chat replay
* restyle search results, category headers, the schedule caption and the "Featured Clips" card
* restyle the Subscribe and Gift a Sub panels, the "Chapters" button and its popup
